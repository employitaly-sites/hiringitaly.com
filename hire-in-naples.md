The Campania regional rate starts high too. For the same gross, a Naples employee usually takes home less than one in Milan or Bologna. Lower housing costs make the overall picture better than the tax line alone suggests.</p>  </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="practical-notes">Practical notes</h2> <ul>  <li><b>Commuting.</b> Metro lines, funiculars and the Circumvesuviana regional railway.
