<ul> <li><strong>Wrong CCNL or job level:</strong> pay below the agreement's minimum, or missing allowances it grants.</li> <li><strong>Part-time and fixed-term contracts:</strong> contributions and leave calculated as for full-time.</li> <li><strong>Late F24 payments:</strong> penalties and interest from day one.</li> <li><strong>Forgotten accruals:</strong> 13th and 14th month and TFR not budgeted, so year-end costs come as a surprise.</li> <li><strong>Mismatched calendars:</strong> Italian payroll closes on the calendar month, which may not match your fiscal periods.</li> <li><strong>Missed changes:</strong> tax brackets, contribution rates and reporting formats change every year.</li> </ul> </section> <section id="reporting" class="prose"> <h2
