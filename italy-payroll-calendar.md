Each month the employer calculates gross pay under the employee's collective agreement, withholds the employee's taxes and contributions, adds the employer's own contributions, pays the employee and pays the authorities.</p> <p>Before the first payslip, the employer must be set up with three bodies:</p> <ul> <li><strong>INPS</strong>, the national social security institute: pensions, unemployment, sickness and maternity.</li> <li><strong>INAIL</strong>, the workplace injury insurer.</li> <li><strong>Agenzia delle Entrate</strong>, the tax agency, for income tax withholding.</li> </ul> <p>The hire itself is reported to the labor authorities electronically the day before work starts.</p> <p>With our Employer of Record service all of these registrations are already in place: we are the employer, so your hire can start without you registering anything in Italy.</p> </section> <section id="cycle" class="prose"> <h2 id="the-monthly-payroll-calendar">The monthly payroll calendar</h2> <p>Each pay period is one calendar month. A typical month looks like this:</p> <ol class="steps"> <li><strong>Mid-month</strong>Cut-off for the month's variables: overtime, absences, bonuses, new hires and leavers.</li> <li><strong>Third week</strong>Payroll is calculated and the payslips are checked.</li> <li><strong>End of month</strong>Net pay is transferred; some employers pay in the first days of the next month, as the contract or CCNL allows. Payslips go out, usually digitally.</li> <li><strong>16th of next month</strong>Withheld taxes, INPS and INAIL contributions are paid with the F24, and the UniEmens report is filed.</li> </ol> <p>Your invoice follows the same calendar, so the cost you see each month matches that month's payroll.</p> </section> <section id="workflow" class="prose"> <h2 id="how-we-run-each-payroll-month">How we run each payroll month</h2> <ol class="steps"> <li><strong>Collect changes</strong>Hours, leave, overtime, new hires and leavers, salary and benefit changes, approved by you.</li> <li><strong>Calculate</strong>Gross pay, contributions, taxes and net pay under the CCNL and the law.</li> <li><strong>Check</strong>Comparison with the previous month; anomalies explained before anything is paid.</li> <li><strong>Pay</strong>Net salaries by bank transfer, payslips to employees.</li> <li><strong>File</strong>F24, UniEmens and the LUL update.</li> <li><strong>Report</strong>Summary and invoice to your finance team.</li> </ol> </section> <section id="filings" class="prose"> <h2 id="monthly-and-yearly-filings">Monthly and yearly filings</h2> <div class="tbl"><table> <thead><tr><th>Filing</th><th>What it is</th><th>When</th></tr></thead> <tbody> <tr><td><b>Payslip</b> (cedolino)</td><td>Monthly statement of gross pay, deductions and net pay</td><td>Every month</td></tr> <tr><td><b>F24</b></td><td>Single payment form for withheld taxes, social security and surcharges</td><td>By the 16th of the following month</td></tr> <tr><td><b>LUL</b> (Libro Unico del Lavoro)</td><td>The official payroll and attendance register</td><td>Updated every month</td></tr> <tr><td><b>UniEmens</b></td><td>Monthly report of pay and contributions to INPS</td><td>Every month</td></tr> <tr><td><b>Certificazione Unica</b> (CU)</td><td>Annual certificate of income, tax and contributions;
