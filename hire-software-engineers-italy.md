# Hire Software Engineers in Italy

> Hiring developers in Italy: CCNL, typical roles, where engineers live, remote work, and what a software engineer costs.


<section> <span class="eyebrow">Hiring by sector</span> <h1 id="hire-software-engineers-in-italy">Hire software engineers in Italy</h1> <p class="lede">Italy has a large pool of engineers trained in strong technical universities, many already working remotely for foreign companies. Most of your hires will be fully remote, and that changes how you set up the contract.</p> </section> <section class="grid"> <div class="panel"><h3 id="usual-ccnl">Usual CCNL</h3><p>Commerce and services (Terziario) or Metalworking (Metalmeccanico) for tech companies. We pick the one that matches your activity and the role.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="typical-roles">Typical roles</h3><p>Backend and frontend developers, mobile, DevOps and SRE, data engineers, ML engineers, QA, engineering managers.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="where-they-are">Where they are</h3><p>Milan, Turin, Rome, Bologna, Pisa and Florence, Naples. Many senior engineers have moved to smaller towns and work remotely.</p></div> </section>  <section class="prose"> <h2 id="what-to-know">What to know</h2> <ul> <li><b>Remote work needs a written agreement.</b> Italy calls it "lavoro agile". We add it to the contract, with rules on equipment and disconnection.</li> <li><b>Equipment.</b> You can ship a laptop or let us reimburse one. Either way it is documented, so it is not taxed as salary.</li> <li><b>Intellectual property.</b> Code written under an employment contract belongs to the employer. We include an explicit IP and confidentiality clause that assigns it to your company.</li>  <li><b>On-call and overtime.</b> On-call duty is paid under the CCNL. Senior engineers can be hired as "quadro" with a flat overtime arrangement.</li> <li><b>Competition for seniors.</b> Good engineers often get offers from other foreign companies. Salary in euros, clear career levels and meal vouchers help.</li> </ul> </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="cost-example">Cost example</h2>  <div class="cta"><a class="btn" href="calculator.html">Open the calculator</a><a class="btn ghost" href="contact.html">Hire an engineer</a></div> </section>
