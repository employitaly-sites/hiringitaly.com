What a US employer needs to know about Italy

CCNLEvery job falls under a national collective agreement that sets minimum pay, hours, leave and notice. We pick the right one for the role.
13thSalaries are paid in 13 monthly installments, sometimes 14. Offers are stated as annual gross pay (RAL).
6.91%Severance pay (TFR) accrues every year and is paid when employment ends, whatever the reason (Civil Code art. 2120).
4 weeksMinimum paid annual leave by law; many CCNLs grant more.
No at-willDismissal needs a valid reason and follows set procedures. A probation period, usually up to 6 months, is the time to assess fit.

Questions US companies ask

Does my company need to register in Italy?

No. We are the legal employer in Italy. You sign one service agreement with our US company and pay one invoice in USD.

Can I convert an Italian contractor into an employee?

Yes, and it reduces the risk that the contract is reclassified as hidden employment. We move contractors and employees from other providers within about 30 days.

When should I open my own Italian entity instead?

Usually once you have a stable team or local sales. We help with the switch and transfer the employees.
