For the same gross, take-home pay is close to Milan and above Rome and Naples. Upper regional brackets still need checking before you compare senior salaries.</p>  </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="practical-notes">Practical notes</h2> <ul>  <li><b>Commuting.</b> Buses and suburban rail. The airport has European flights.</li>  <li><b>Time zones.</b> Italy is UTC+1, UTC+2 in summer: 6 hours ahead of New York and 9 ahead of San Francisco.
