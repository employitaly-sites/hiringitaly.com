# For Employees Hired Through HiringItaly

> For people hired in Italy through HiringItaly: your Italian contract, payslip, INPS, TFR, 13th month, leave, and answers to common questions.


<section> <span class="eyebrow">For employees</span> <h1 id="you-have-been-hired-through-hiringitaly">You have been hired through HiringItaly</h1> <p class="lede">A company abroad wants you on its team and has asked us to employ you in Italy. You work for them. We make sure you are paid and protected like any employee in Italy.</p> </section> <section class="grid"> <div class="panel"><h3 id="italian-contract">Italian contract</h3><p>A written employment contract under the CCNL for your sector, with your level, salary, hours and probation period.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="monthly-payslip">Monthly payslip</h3><p>Paid every month, with tax and contributions already withheld. You get a clear payslip you can use for loans or rentals.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="inps-contributions">INPS contributions</h3><p>Your pension and social security build up in Italy. You are covered for sickness, maternity and unemployment.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="tfr">TFR</h3><p>About 6.91% of your yearly pay is set aside as severance and paid when you leave, or moved to a pension fund if you choose.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="13th-month">13th month</h3><p>An extra month's pay in December. Your CCNL may add a 14th in summer.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="leave">Leave</h3><p>At least 4 weeks of paid vacation, public holidays, paid sick leave, maternity and paternity leave.</p></div> </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="questions-employees-ask">Questions employees ask</h2> <h3 id="who-is-my-employer">Who is my employer?</h3><p>EmployItaly is your legal employer in Italy. You work day to day for the company that hired you: they set your tasks, team and goals. We handle your contract, payslip, taxes and contributions.</p><h3 id="is-this-a-real-italian-employment-contract">Is this a real Italian employment contract?</h3><p>Yes. Same contract type, same national collective agreement (CCNL) and same rights as any employee of an Italian company.</p><h3 id="when-do-i-get-paid">When do I get paid?</h3><p>Every month, by bank transfer to your Italian or EU account, with a payslip. The pay date is in your contract.</p><h3 id="do-i-pay-taxes-myself">Do I pay taxes myself?</h3><p>No. Income tax and your share of contributions are withheld from your pay. Every year you receive the CU certificate. You file a tax return only if you have other income or want to claim deductions.</p><h3 id="does-my-pension-build-up">Does my pension build up?</h3><p>Yes. Contributions go to INPS like for any Italian employee and count toward your pension.</p><h3 id="what-happens-when-i-leave">What happens when I leave?</h3><p>You receive your TFR, unused leave and the accrued share of the 13th month. If you are dismissed you may be entitled to unemployment benefit (NASpI) under the usual rules.</p><h3 id="can-i-work-from-home">Can I work from home?</h3><p>Yes, if agreed with the company. We put the remote work agreement in writing.</p><h3 id="who-do-i-call-if-something-is-wrong-with-my-payslip">Who do I call if something is wrong with my payslip?</h3><p>Us. You get a direct contact in our payroll team, in Italian or English.</p><h3 id="what-if-i-am-not-an-eu-citizen">What if I am not an EU citizen?</h3><p>We handle the work permit and visa together with your employer.</p> </section> <section> <p class="prose">Still talking with a company abroad?
