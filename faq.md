# FAQ for US Companies

> 25 answers for US companies hiring in Italy through an Employer of Record: process, costs, employment rules, equity, benefits and data.


<section> <span class="eyebrow">FAQ</span> <h1 id="questions-us-companies-ask-us">Questions US companies ask us</h1> <p class="lede">Short answers. For anything specific to your role, ask us directly.</p> </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="how-it-works">How it works</h2> <h3 id="what-is-an-employer-of-record">What is an Employer of Record?</h3><p>A company that legally employs someone on your behalf. We hold the Italian employment contract and run payroll. You direct the work.</p><h3 id="do-we-need-an-italian-entity">Do we need an Italian entity?</h3><p>No. That is the point of the service.</p><h3 id="who-manages-the-employee-day-to-day">Who manages the employee day to day?</h3><p>You do: tasks, schedule, goals, tools, reviews.</p><h3 id="how-fast-can-someone-start">How fast can someone start?</h3><p>Usually within days of a signed offer for EU citizens. Visa cases take longer, depending on the permit.</p><h3 id="can-we-convert-an-existing-contractor">Can we convert an existing contractor?</h3><p>Yes. We move them to an employment contract, typically within 30 days.</p><h3 id="can-we-hire-part-time-or-fixed-term">Can we hire part-time or fixed-term?</h3><p>Yes. Both are standard in Italy, with limits on fixed-term duration and renewals.</p> </section><section class="prose"> <h2 id="costs-and-billing">Costs and billing</h2> <h3 id="what-does-it-cost">What does it cost?</h3><p>€250 per employee per month, plus the employment cost billed monthly on actuals.</p><h3 id="what-is-in-the-employment-cost">What is in the employment cost?</h3><p>Gross salary, employer social contributions, 13th month (and 14th where applicable), TFR, CCNL funds, and any benefits or expenses you approve.</p><h3 id="what-currency-do-you-invoice-in">What currency do you invoice in?</h3><p>US dollars, from our US company.</p><h3 id="how-much-more-than-salary-does-an-employee-cost">How much more than salary does an employee cost?</h3><p>It depends on salary and CCNL. Employer contributions are around 30%, plus 13th month and TFR. Use the calculator for a specific number.</p><h3 id="is-there-a-deposit">Is there a deposit?</h3><h3 id="what-about-visas">What about visas?</h3><p>€2,500 per case for the work permit and visa process.</p><h3 id="can-you-run-payroll-if-we-already-have-an-italian-company">Can you run payroll if we already have an Italian company?</h3><p>Yes, at €55 per payslip.</p> </section><section class="prose"> <h2 id="employment-rules">Employment rules</h2> <h3 id="can-we-terminate-at-will">Can we terminate at will?</h3><p>No. Dismissal needs a valid reason and a procedure. Probation, up to 6 months depending on the CCNL, is the period when either side can end it freely.</p><h3 id="how-much-vacation-do-employees-get">How much vacation do employees get?</h3><p>At least 4 weeks a year, plus public holidays. CCNLs often add more paid hours off.</p><h3 id="do-we-need-to-provide-health-insurance">Do we need to provide health insurance?</h3><p>No. Italy has a national health service funded by taxes. Some CCNLs include a supplementary health fund, which is part of the cost.</p><h3 id="what-is-the-tfr">What is the TFR?</h3><p>Severance pay set aside every year (about 6.91% of pay) and paid when employment ends.</p><h3 id="what-is-a-ccnl">What is a CCNL?</h3><p>The national collective agreement for the sector. It sets minimum pay, levels, hours, notice and many benefits.</p><h3 id="can-we-include-a-non-compete">Can we include a non-compete?</h3><p>Yes, if it is written, paid, and limited in time, place and scope.</p><h3 id="who-owns-the-employee-s-work">Who owns the employee&#39;s work?</h3><p>Your company. Our contracts include an IP assignment and confidentiality clause.</p> </section><section class="prose"> <h2 id="equity-benefits-and-data">Equity, benefits and data</h2> <h3 id="can-the-employee-join-our-stock-plan">Can the employee join our stock plan?</h3><h3 id="which-benefits-are-common">Which benefits are common?</h3><p>Meal vouchers (tax-free up to €10 a day if electronic), company car, supplementary pension and health, and fringe benefits up to €1,000 a year (€2,000 with dependent children).</p><h3 id="where-is-employee-data-stored">Where is employee data stored?</h3><p>On our servers in the EU, under GDPR.</p><h3 id="can-the-employee-work-from-another-country">Can the employee work from another country?</h3><p>Short trips are fine. Working from another country for longer changes where tax and contributions are due, so tell us first.</p><h3 id="what-happens-if-we-later-open-our-own-entity">What happens if we later open our own entity?</h3><p>We transfer employees to it without losing seniority, and help with the company setup through our partners.</p> </section><section> <div class="cta"><a class="btn" href="contact.html">Ask a question</a><a class="btn ghost" href="calculator.html">Estimate a cost</a></div> </section>
