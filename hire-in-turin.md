Low earners pay 0.8%, but the rate rises to 1.2% on the top bracket. The exemption is the lowest of the five. Senior engineers and managers in Turin pay more city surcharge than in Milan or Bologna at the same gross. Lower housing costs often balance this.</p>  </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="practical-notes">Practical notes</h2> <ul>  <li><b>Commuting.</b> One metro line plus trams, buses and a suburban rail link. The city is flat and compact, and many people cycle.</li> <li><b>Travel.</b> High-speed trains reach Milan in about one hour. Turin airport has European flights; for the US, connect through a hub or Milan Malpensa.</li>  <li><b>Time zones.</b> Italy is UTC+1, UTC+2 in summer: 6 hours ahead of New York and 9 ahead of San Francisco. A team working 9:00 to 18:00 has three shared hours with the East Coast (9:00 to 12:00 New York time).
