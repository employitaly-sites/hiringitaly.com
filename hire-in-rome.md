Most professional salaries sit in the bracket where the 3.33% regional rate applies. For the same gross, a Rome employee usually takes home less than one in Milan or Bologna. If you pay the same salary across cities, Rome staff will notice.</p>  </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="practical-notes">Practical notes</h2> <ul>  <li><b>Commuting.</b> The metro network is small for the size of the city, and traffic is heavy. Office location matters: pick a site near a metro or regional rail station and allow hybrid work.</li> <li><b>Travel.</b> Fiumicino is the largest Italian airport, with direct flights to several US cities.</li>  <li><b>Time zones.</b> Italy is UTC+1, UTC+2 in summer: 6 hours ahead of New York and 9 ahead of San Francisco. A team working 9:00 to 18:00 has three shared hours with the East Coast (9:00 to 12:00 New York time).
