# Hire in Milan, Italy

> Hiring employees in Milan: main sectors, universities, local income tax surcharges, take-home pay and practical notes for US companies.


<section> <span class="eyebrow">Hiring by city</span> <h1 id="hire-in-milan">Hire in Milan</h1> <p class="lede">Milan is where most foreign companies open their first Italian seat. Finance, fashion, consulting and tech all compete for the same people, so expect a fast market and firm salary expectations.</p> </section> <section class="grid"> <div class="panel"><h3 id="main-sectors">Main sectors</h3><p>Banking and asset management, fashion and luxury, industrial design, consulting, media, pharma and the regional head offices of most multinationals. A growing startup scene around fintech and SaaS.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="talent-pools">Talent pools</h3><p>Politecnico di Milano for engineering and design, Bocconi for economics and management, the Università Statale for law, sciences and medicine, Bicocca for sciences and statistics.</p></div> </section> <section> <h2 id="local-income-tax-in-milan">Local income tax in Milan</h2> <p class="prose">On top of national IRPEF, employees pay a city surcharge and a regional surcharge. People on lower salaries pay no city surcharge here, which they would pay in Rome or Naples. On mid and senior salaries the 0.8% rate is among the lowest of the large cities, and the Lombardy regional rate starts low too. For the same gross, a Milan employee usually takes home a little more than one in Rome or Naples.</p>  </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="practical-notes">Practical notes</h2> <ul>  <li><b>Commuting.</b> Five metro lines, suburban rail and trams. Many staff live outside the city and come in by train from Monza, Bergamo, Pavia or Novara.</li> <li><b>Travel.</b> Linate is close to the centre. Malpensa has direct flights to New York and other US hubs.</li>  <li><b>Time zones.</b> Italy is UTC+1, UTC+2 in summer: 6 hours ahead of New York and 9 ahead of San Francisco. A team working 9:00 to 18:00 has three shared hours with the East Coast (9:00 to 12:00 New York time). With the West Coast there is no overlap in a standard day, so plan a late afternoon slot in Italy or async work.</li> </ul> </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="start-hiring-in-milan">Start hiring in Milan</h2> <p>Estimate the full employer cost, then tell us about the role.</p> <div class="cta"><a class="btn" href="calculator.html">Open the calculator</a><a class="btn ghost" href="contact.html">Get a quote</a></div> </section>
